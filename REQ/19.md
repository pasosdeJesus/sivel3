# R-#19: Send the 5% church tithe to learn.tg's church fund

sivel3 transfers 5% of the income that reaches a region's donation pool to the
**church fund of learn.tg**, and does **not** implement anything about churches.
The church directory, the verification of churches and pastors, and the
distribution of the fund (proportional to the square root of verified members,
capped at 20% per church, per `PRINCIPLES.md`) live in learn.tg.

## Decision (2026-11-21)

- **One destination, not one per church.** sivel3 sends the 5% to learn.tg's
  church fund address for the active network (`celo` / `celoSepolia`); it never
  resolves an individual church.
- **No donor-church choice in the MVP.** The earlier wording ("donors can
  optionally direct 5% to a verified church") is out of scope: the tithe is
  automatic and identical for every donation.
- **learn.tg owns the domain.** sivel3 does not duplicate the church directory
  (`learn.tg` already exposes `/api/directory/churches`), does not verify
  pastors, and does not decide the split among churches.
- **sivel3 owns the transfer and its audit.** It records the transfer (hash,
  amount, network) so the 5% appears in the public transparency surface.

## Base of calculation

The percentages in `VISION.md` §6 apply to the net income that reaches the
regional pool: each donation first sends 10% to the SLEARN reserve, and the
remaining 90% is assigned to `SIVeL3RegionalDonationV2`. The church tithe is 5%
of that pool (plus 5% of subscription and credit revenue), not 5% of the gross
donation.

## Scope

**In scope (sivel3):**

1. Configuration: learn.tg church fund address per network, with the same
   resolution rules as the other contracts (`apps/contracts/deployments/`, then
   the canonical default; never a bare `process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_*` read).
2. A transfer of 5% when the regional pool is settled (or when a donation is
   assigned, if the pool-level settlement is not implemented yet), signed with
   the backend wallet and non-blocking: a failure must not lose the donation.
3. An audit row and a public read (transparency endpoint or existing
   analytics surface) with amount, network, destination and transaction hash.
4. Idempotence: one transfer per source transaction; a retry must not pay twice.

**Out of scope (learn.tg):** directory, church/pastor verification, reputation,
distribution rules, and the fund's governance. Those require their own issue in
the learn.tg repository.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A donation of 10 USDT assigns 9 USDT to the region and sends 0.45 USDT to
      the learn.tg church fund (5% of the 90%).
- [ ] The transfer is visible with its hash and is not duplicated on retry.
- [ ] With the fund address unset for a network, the donation still succeeds and
      the tithe is recorded as `pending`, never silently dropped.
- [ ] No church, pastor or directory data is stored, fetched or rendered in
      sivel3.
