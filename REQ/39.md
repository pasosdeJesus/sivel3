## Motivation

After a USDT donation, sivel.xyz mints SLEARN directly on Celo as **cashback** for all donors. The donation is split: 90% to the region's RegionalDonation contract, 10% to the SLEARN reserve.

## Flow

```
User donates 100 USDT → sivel.xyz backend wallet
  │
  ▼
POST /api/donations/assign
  ├── Verify on-chain transfer
  ├── 10 USDT → SLEARN contract → mintAndReserve(donor, 10 USDT)
  │     → 220 SLEARN minted to donor
  ├── 90 USDT → RegionalDonation contract → assignDonation()
  └── Record transaction
```

## Key rules

| Condition | SLEARN cashback | USDT to region |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Any donor (verified or not) | 10% of donation → SLEARN reserve → mint SLEARN | 90% of USDT |

> The 10% USDT goes to the SLEARN contract reserve and `mintAndReserve()` is called —
> USDT stays in reserve backing the minted SLEARN. The donor receives 22 SLEARN per USDT reserved.

> **Why all donors?** SLEARN is an incentive to explore the ecosystem. Verification on learn.tg is encouraged
> via the UI (toast message differs for verified vs unverified), but not required for cashback.
> The donation itself is proof of good faith.
